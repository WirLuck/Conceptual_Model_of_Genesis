# 03 — Mathematical Formalization v0.1 / Formalisasi Matematis v0.1

> **Status:** provisional mathematical scaffolding. These expressions organize the hypothesis; they are not established laws of physics.

## 1. Symbols / Simbol

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `P` | Primordial Substance |
| `C_P` | primordial consciousness |
| `Σ_n` | model state at logical/computational ordering index `n` |
| `D` | differentiation operator / candidate transition law |
| `G` | generative mapping |
| `R_n` | relational structure |
| `S,T` | emergent spatial and temporal structure |
| `g` | emergent geometry/metric |
| `E_phys` | physical energy in the emergent physical regime |
| `M` | matter/fields/physical degrees of freedom |
| `C_i` | individual consciousness |
| `Ω_i` | organized physical/informational structure associated with individual `i` |
| `Λ` | unknown lawful parameters |

## 2. Ontological existence / Eksistensi ontologis

**[PSESH]**

\[
\operatorname{Exists}(P)=1
\]

This is a symbolic existence predicate. It does **not** mean that P has been mathematically defined as a set, field, manifold, or physical substance.

## 3. Relative absence / Ketiadaan relatif

**[PSESH]** Define:

\[
N(X\mid F)=1
\]

when X is absent relative to frame/domain F.

PSESH's ontological proposition is that statements such as `matter = 0`, `spatial extension = 0`, or `X is absent` do not entail `P = 0` or an instantiated state of absolute non-being.

## 4. Primordial state / Keadaan primordial

**[PSESH][SPEC]**

\[
\Sigma_P=(P,C_P,\Lambda)
\]

This notation packages the current foundational commitments without assuming ordinary spacetime coordinates.

## 5. Primordial consciousness / Kesadaran primordial

**[PSESH]**

\[
C_P\in\operatorname{Intrinsic}(P)
\]

This states the project's foundational-consciousness hypothesis. It is not an empirical result.

A crucial distinction is:

\[
E_{\mathrm{phys}}\neq C_P
\]

Known biological conscious processing depends on physical energy, but that does not establish an identity between consciousness and energy, nor does it license importing ordinary physical energy into a pre-spatiotemporal description.

## 6. Generative relevance / Relevansi generatif

If `C_P` is claimed to have a real role in creation rather than being a redundant label, the model eventually needs a discriminating consequence. A strong counterfactual requirement is:

**[PSESH][OPEN]**

\[
G[P,C_P;\Lambda]\neq G[P,\neg C_P;\Lambda]
\]

This is currently a **research requirement**, not a measured result. A future theory must specify what differs and how the difference could in principle be detected or constrained.

## 7. Differentiation / Diferensiasi

**[SPEC]** Candidate state-transition form:

\[
\Sigma_{n+1}=D(\Sigma_n;P,C_P,\Lambda)
\]

The index `n` denotes logical/computational ordering. It must **not** automatically be interpreted as primordial physical time.

A candidate differentiation measure may later be introduced:

\[
\Delta_n=\operatorname{Diff}(\Sigma_n)
\]

with, over a monotonic creation regime if justified,

\[
\Delta_{n+1}\geq\Delta_n.
\]

No particular definition of `Diff` has yet been selected.

## 8. Emergent relations and geometry / Relasi dan geometri emergen

**[SPEC]**

\[
R_n\xrightarrow{\mathcal E}(M,g)
\]

A future relational model may define graph distance as:

\[
d_G(i,j)=\min_{p:i\to j}|p|
\]

and investigate whether neighborhoods obey approximately

\[
N(r)\propto r^{d_{\mathrm{eff}}},
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx\frac{d\ln N(r)}{d\ln r}.
\]

These are candidate tools for studying emergent dimensionality, **not laws of creation**.

## 9. Physical regime / Rezim fisik

Only after a physical/spatiotemporal regime has emerged do ordinary physical quantities such as energy become safely applicable:

\[
(M,g)\Rightarrow E_{\mathrm{phys}},\;\text{fields},\;\text{matter},\ldots
\]

PSESH currently has no derivation of the Standard Model, Einstein field equations, quantum fields, or a specific cosmological metric.

## 10. Individual consciousness / Kesadaran individual

**[SPEC][OPEN]** Current placeholder:

\[
C_i=\mathcal I(C_P,\Omega_i)
\]

This expression records the individuation problem; it does not solve it. `𝓘` is unknown. A successful theory must explain why multiple organized systems correspond to distinct centers of experience if primordial consciousness is fundamental.

## 11. Boundary / Batas

If ordinary space is emergent, a primordial spatial boundary is not currently defined:

\[
\partial P\;\text{undefined in the present model}.
\]

Likewise, if physical time is emergent, expressions such as `t_start(P)` and `t_end(P)` are not presently well-defined.

## 12. What is still missing / Yang masih belum ada

The formal program is incomplete until PSESH supplies at least: a mathematical definition of `P`; an explicit `D`/`G`; an emergence map `𝓔` that yields definite geometry; a mechanism or formal role for `C_P`; a derivation or interface to physical energy/matter; and predictions distinguishing PSESH from matched alternatives.

Until then, the equations above are a **formal research scaffold**, not a completed physical theory.
