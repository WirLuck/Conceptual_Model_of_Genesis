The **Generative Principle** is the research position connecting PSESM's primordial propositions with differentiation and development toward physical reality. PSESM does not yet know the physical or mathematical form of this principle.

This is the primary location for testing whether established mathematics, physical laws, and mature sciences are compatible with the model without forcing them to fit.

## Metode kompatibilitas / Compatibility method

Untuk setiap teori, hukum, atau struktur matematika yang dikaji, hasil sementara harus diklasifikasikan sebagai / For every theory, law, or mathematical structure examined, the provisional result should be classified as:

- **Compatible / Kompatibel** — tidak ditemukan kontradiksi pada ruang lingkup yang diuji.
- **Incompatible / Tidak kompatibel** — terdapat konflik yang dapat dinyatakan dengan jelas.
- **Irrelevant / Tidak relevan** — teori tidak menjawab bagian model yang sedang diuji.
- **Requires clarification/modification / Memerlukan klarifikasi/modifikasi** — hubungan belum dapat ditentukan tanpa definisi atau derivasi tambahan.

**Compatibility is not evidence. / Kompatibilitas bukan bukti.** Sebuah teori mapan yang dapat hidup berdampingan dengan PSESM tidak dengan sendirinya mendukung klaim primordial consciousness atau will.

## Invariants, states, and constraints — research question

🇮🇩 Salah satu arah yang layak diuji adalah membedakan **apa yang berubah** selama diferensiasi dari **apa yang tetap/invariant**. PSESM belum menyatakan bahwa P adalah konstanta matematika. Yang dipertahankan saat ini hanyalah status ontologis P sebagai realitas primordial/fundamental. Apakah status tersebut kelak memiliki representasi sebagai mathematical invariant atau conservation principle tetap merupakan pertanyaan terbuka.

🇬🇧 One useful direction is to distinguish **what changes** during differentiation from **what remains invariant**. PSESM does not currently state that P is a mathematical constant. The retained claim is only P's ontological status as primordial/fundamental reality. Whether this can later be represented as a mathematical invariant or conservation principle remains open.

## Relativity / Relativitas

**[EST][LIT-relevant]** Relativity is a conceptual pivot rather than evidence for PSESM. It concerns observer/reference-frame-dependent temporal measurement and relations, while PSESM separately asks an ontological question about the existence of time. Observer consciousness is not what produces relativistic time dilation.

## Kriteria kemajuan / Progress criterion

Kemajuan yang bermakna bukan sekadar menemukan persamaan yang dapat ditafsirkan cocok dengan PSESM. / Meaningful progress is not merely finding equations that can be interpreted as fitting PSESM.

A stronger result would be one or more of:

1. a clear mathematical representation derived from defined concepts;
2. a non-trivial constraint imposed by the model;
3. an identified contradiction requiring revision;
4. a correspondence that explains something not inserted by assumption;
5. a distinctive prediction that differs from an alternative model and can in principle be tested.

Until such results exist, the Generative Principle remains **[OPEN]** and proposed causal connections remain **[PSESM][SPEC]**.
